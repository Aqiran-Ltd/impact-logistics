Impact Medical Logistics - Internal Driver & Rep App
Blueprint
Purpose
A streamlined internal application designed for Impact Medical Logistics drivers and reps. The
app tracks trays and bins, documents pickup and delivery locations, captures photo proof, and
allows reps to request office pickup once items are ready.
DRIVER APP (Internal Only)

1. Driver Login
   ▪ Secure login
   ▪ Daily assigned route
   ▪ Driver identity tied to all actions
2. Pickup Module (Trays & Bins)
   Drivers record:
   ▪ Hospital or Surgery Center Name
   ▪ Team Name (e.g., Ortho, Cardio, GI, Lab)
   ▪ Item Type: Trays or Bins
   ▪ Quantity: Number of trays and bins
   ▪ Pickup Photo
   ▪ Timestamp
   ▪ Optional notes
   Result: Reps instantly see pickup details including quantities and photo proof.
3. Delivery Module (Photo Proof Only)
   Drivers record:
   ▪ Delivery Location (Hospital or Surgery Center)
   ▪ Team Delivered To
